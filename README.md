<a id="key-features"></a>

### Core Functionality:
- **Multi-Track Recording** — Effortlessly record and mix multiple tracks for a polished sound.
- **Comping Feature** — Combine recorded tracks to achieve the best sound, perfect for live performances.
- **Extensive Sound Library** — Access a vast collection of instruments and samples to enhance your musical projects.
- **User-Friendly Interface** — Customize your workspace for maximum productivity and creativity.
